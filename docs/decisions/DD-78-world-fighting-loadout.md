# DD-78: World battles use the player's most recently saved loadout that is legal right now (rechecked against level and collection), else a legal loadout built from the player's own collection (the element with the most owned abilities, the best owned capacity item that fits, then owned abilities)

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 7.4, 10.2, 10.3, R-LOAD-004

## Decision

World battles use the player's most recently saved loadout that is legal right now (rechecked against level and collection), else a legal loadout built from the player's own collection (the element with the most owned abilities, the best owned capacity item that fits, then owned abilities). Lesson battles use the lesson's fixed loadouts at the lesson's level with the player as White; other world battles assign colours at random.

## Why it is the best case

A new player can battle before they ever open the loadout builder (M5 first win in under 30 minutes) and nobody fights with modules they do not own (R-LOAD-004).
