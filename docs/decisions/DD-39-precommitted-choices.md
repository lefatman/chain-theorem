# DD-39: Choices pre-supplied with a move (mv

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 5.3, 13.4

## Decision

Choices pre-supplied with a move (mv.choices) are used only for the mover's own CAPTURING abilities in the committed action; every other choice is prompted.

## Why it is the best case

5.3 Commit: the mover makes the choices their own CAPTURING abilities need; nothing else can be known in advance.
