# DD-38: Format objectives count captures of pieces by their current type (a promoted pawn counts as its new type) and credit an effect capture to the side owning the ability that caused it

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 9.1, E4

## Decision

Format objectives count captures of pieces by their current type (a promoted pawn counts as its new type) and credit an effect capture to the side owning the ability that caused it.

## Why it is the best case

9.1 counts 'any non-pawn piece (move or effect capture)'; E4 credits the Poisoned Meat pawn's owner.
