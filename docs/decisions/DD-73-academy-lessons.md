# DD-73: Chess Academy lessons: puzzle goals are capture (movement), give check (check) and mate (checkmate), and a puzzle's accepted moves must be every legal move that reaches the goal

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 10.3, R-WORLD-003

## Decision

Chess Academy lessons: puzzle goals are capture (movement), give check (check) and mate (checkmate), and a puzzle's accepted moves must be every legal move that reaches the goal. Battle lessons are First Blood from the standard start with fixed loaner loadouts legal at their levels, against a Wild-tier sparring army that may carry no ability; ability lessons use one element on both sides so nothing is silenced, and the element lesson gives the player the advantaged element. A lesson battle grants only the lesson's reward, never battle XP.

## Why it is the best case

Each lesson teaches exactly one idea (10.3); a correct answer is never marked wrong; loaner loadouts let a level-1 player feel an ability before owning it.
