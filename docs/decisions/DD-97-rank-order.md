# DD-97: Rank order for abilities that compare pieces: pawn 1 < knight 2 = bishop 2 < rook 3 < queen 4 < king 5; 'equal or higher' includes the knight/bishop tie

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 5.1, 5.8

## Decision

Rank order for abilities that compare pieces: pawn 1 < knight 2 = bishop 2 < rook 3 < queen 4 < king 5; 'equal or higher' includes the knight/bishop tie. Offensive abilities are the CAPTURING and CAPTURES categories.

## Why it is the best case

Designer's answer (2026-10-06) to the catalogue plan; classic piece values keep the comparison intuitive and the tie makes the minor pieces interchangeable for Necromancer, Quantum Kill, Obstinate and Redo.
