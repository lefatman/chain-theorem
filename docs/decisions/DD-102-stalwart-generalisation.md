# DD-102: Stalwart generalisation details: Poisoned Meat's effect capture carries a 'venom' tag that Stalwart's protection does not stop; Stalwart pieces bypass capture and move restrictions from passives (Obstinate, Hot Foot burns, later passives) but not Block Path; a loadout is invalid when a piece type's set holds Stalwart together with any CAPTURING or CAPTURES ability; the king keeps 4

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 4.3, 5.8, R-RULES-003

## Decision

Stalwart generalisation details: Poisoned Meat's effect capture carries a 'venom' tag that Stalwart's protection does not stop; Stalwart pieces bypass capture and move restrictions from passives (Obstinate, Hot Foot burns, later passives) but not Block Path; a loadout is invalid when a piece type's set holds Stalwart together with any CAPTURING or CAPTURES ability; the king keeps 4.3 exactly.

## Why it is the best case

Designer answer 4; a tag keeps the Poisoned Meat exception data-driven instead of naming a module in the engine, and the loadout rule enforces 'cannot equip offensive abilities' at build time where players can see it.
