# DD-17: Charges are spent only when at least one effect of the activation resolves (a fully fizzled, negated or silenced activation costs nothing); a consumable ability with no charges left does not trigger at all

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 5.4, 5.7, E9

## Decision

Charges are spent only when at least one effect of the activation resolves (a fully fizzled, negated or silenced activation costs nothing); a consumable ability with no charges left does not trigger at all.

## Why it is the best case

Keeps consumables calculable (pillar 1) and matches E9, where Rebirth returns 'each time its starting square is empty'.
