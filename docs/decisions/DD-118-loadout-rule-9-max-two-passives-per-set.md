# DD-118: validateLoadout gains rule 9 of spec 7.4: for every ability set it counts the distinct ids whose module declares category 'PASSIVE' and, when the count exceeds caps.MAX_PASSIVES_PER_SET (2, PLAYTEST, packages/content/config.ts), reports { rule: 9, code: 'passives_exceeded', message: 'the <label> holds N passive abilities; at most 2 may share a set', ref: <the first passive over the cap> } after rule 8's errors

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 7.4, 13.5, R-LOAD-004, R-LOAD-003, R-ABIL-005, DD-102, DD-111, docs/BALANCE_BASELINE.md 5, 6 (finding 3), 9.3, 11; docs/BALANCE_DD98.md 3, 4

## Decision

validateLoadout gains rule 9 of spec 7.4: for every ability set it counts the distinct ids whose module declares category 'PASSIVE' and, when the count exceeds caps.MAX_PASSIVES_PER_SET (2, PLAYTEST, packages/content/config.ts), reports { rule: 9, code: 'passives_exceeded', message: 'the <label> holds N passive abilities; at most 2 may share a set', ref: <the first passive over the cap> } after rule 8's errors. The cap is per set, not per loadout: three passives spread over per-type sets are legal, and a repeated passive id stays rule 4's duplicate_ability so the two rules never overlap. The rule reads category data only, so a future passive card is covered without an engine change and `makeEngine({ MAX_PASSIVES_PER_SET: n })` or `pnpm sim --caps` retunes it. LoadoutError['rule'] includes 9 and LoadoutErrorCode 'passives_exceeded'; spec 7.4 lists rule 9 and the CONTENT_GUIDE caps table the knob; the loadout builder's abilityBlock refuses a third passive with 'Set already holds two passives' (the word derived from the cap, shown after the capacity reason) and names rule 9 'passives per set' in its error list; the property suite's independent spec checker and mutation test cover rule 9 and its generator stops dealing passives at the cap.

## Why it is the best case

docs/BALANCE_BASELINE.md finding 3 (section 6): Maximum and Flexible carry a king set of Stalwart, Block Path and Veil (plus Electric Slide) and beat Focused 79% and 85% and Starter 96–100% in Full Battle, losing only to a move capture of the king (stalwart_captured 12 of 144), while Maximum against Flexible sits at 44–56%, so the archetype target is missed only through that king set. Section 5 prices the stacked cards on their own: Stalwart 81.3% and Block Path 72.9% in Full Battle (Veil 41.7%), and section 9.3 finding 2 shows that an item-slot price (DD-109, overruled by DD-111) still left the Schedule builds at 81–88% over Focused with a quarter of games ending in stalwart_captured, naming 'at most two passives per set' as the lever that 'takes Block Path or Veil off the king'; section 6's plan list backs rule 9 for B5 next to Obstinate at two slots (finding 2: Obstinate, 95.8% alone, is the backbone of every Full Battle build). docs/BALANCE_DD98.md section 3 already had Maximum 65–75% ahead in Full Battle after the neutral-first catalogue, and the gap survived B1–B4 while the advantaged-element score fell from 90% to 73%, so it is the king set and not the silence rule. A per-set cap of two is the smallest rule that breaks the three-card king while every 7.3 build stands (the Maximum king set holds exactly Stalwart and Veil) and a Stalwart king with one shield stays a real choice; counting distinct ids keeps rule 9 orthogonal to rule 4 (the mutation test requires each mutation to break exactly one rule), and the check sits after rule 8 so the error list stays in rule order.
