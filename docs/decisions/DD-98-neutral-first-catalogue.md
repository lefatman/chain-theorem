# DD-98: Neutral-first catalogue: every ability is neutral except one signature per element (Tide hit_and_run, Grove poisoned_meat, Ember cleave, Stone stonewall, Frost frost_heave; Storm squall until electric_slide ships)

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 5.7, 6.3, 6.5, 7.2, 17.2, R-ELEM-003

## Decision

Neutral-first catalogue: every ability is neutral except one signature per element (Tide hit_and_run, Grove poisoned_meat, Ember cleave, Stone stonewall, Frost frost_heave; Storm squall until electric_slide ships). Signatures move to levels 1-3. Former attuned bonuses are folded into the base where the base was thin (backdraft, buttress, phalanx, afterimage, snowdrift) and dropped elsewhere; DD-40's attuned item reveals are retired; 6.5's 'at least four affinity abilities' becomes 'exactly one signature'; the Attunement Charm attunes the chosen element's signature.

## Why it is the best case

Designer decision (2026-10-06): element identity belongs to the traits, and stacking attunement on whole affinity sets is why the advantaged element won 64-73% of simulated games against a 55-60% target. Content-only change; silence, traits and Blended Family are untouched.
