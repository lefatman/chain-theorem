# DD-19: Choice options are filtered by public rules only: occupancy, burning squares, Royal Immunity (kings are never offered as effect-capture targets) and the INV-03 simulation

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 5.4, R-SEC-001

## Decision

Choice options are filtered by public rules only: occupancy, burning squares, Royal Immunity (kings are never offered as effect-capture targets) and the INV-03 simulation. Hidden protections (Antidote, Bulwark) are not used to filter, so a chosen effect can still fizzle.

## Why it is the best case

Filtering by hidden protections would leak them through the option list (R-SEC-001).
