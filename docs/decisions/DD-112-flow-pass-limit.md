# DD-112: Flow (Tide, R-ELEM-006; identity COMMITTED) lets a Tide piece pass at most CAPS.TRAITS.FLOW_PASS_LIMIT of its own side's pieces in one move, a PLAYTEST number that ships as 1 (0 restores the launch rule of no limit), for sliding moves, a pawn's two-square first move and attacks alike; the piece still never ends a move on an ally, knights, single king steps and castling are unchanged, and an Electric Slide turn adds its crossings to the same count (the corner itself is not a crossing)

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 6.1, 6.4, R-ELEM-006, R-ELEM-001, DD-104

## Decision

Flow (Tide, R-ELEM-006; identity COMMITTED) lets a Tide piece pass at most CAPS.TRAITS.FLOW_PASS_LIMIT of its own side's pieces in one move, a PLAYTEST number that ships as 1 (0 restores the launch rule of no limit), for sliding moves, a pawn's two-square first move and attacks alike; the piece still never ends a move on an ally, knights, single king steps and castling are unchanged, and an Electric Slide turn adds its crossings to the same count (the corner itself is not a crossing). The engine represents it as the moveFilter.passThrough hook returning boolean or a number (true no limit, false or 0 none, n a per-move cap), stored per piece in MoveRules.passThrough (0 none, 1-254 the cap, PASS_UNLIMITED 255; the largest answer across hooks stands) with the side's anyPass fast path; move generation counts the allies crossed along each ray, slideWithTurns carries the count across turned segments, the double push needs a cap of at least 1, and attacked() and reachesVia() count crossings the same way so checks, pins and king safety agree with the legal moves.

## Why it is the best case

Flow was the strongest trait in First Blood: docs/BALANCE_DD98.md has Tide at 60% over Ember, 67% over Grove (its own foil, so Grove over Tide read 33%, one of three foil pairs pointing the wrong way) and 88% over Frost, with the advantaged-element score down to 46% because the traits decided (Tide's Flow and Stone's Bulwark beat everything), and asked whether to accept the trait-led First Blood picture or tune Flow and Bulwark; docs/BALANCE_BASELINE.md keeps the picture after the Electric Slide fix (only two foil pairs point the right way in First Blood because the traits and the opening override the wheel) and finds Tide beating every element at 60-100% in Full Battle once silence is loosened. Capping Flow at one ally per move keeps the trait's identity and every spec example (the E8 rook through its pawn, the double push over a knight, checks and pins through one ally, the 50-move opening) while removing the free stacking of several allies on one line that gave Tide uncontested development and long-range checks; the number is a config knob measured against the launch rule in section 10 of the baseline.
