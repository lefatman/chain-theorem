# DD-71: Zone presence: players may share a tile; a reconnect within the same channel keeps its position, battle and lesson state and merges progress lists; positions are saved only on logout and zone change (R-COST-002); a full channel (60) sends the player to the next channel of the zone

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 10.1, R-WORLD-001, R-COST-002

## Decision

Zone presence: players may share a tile; a reconnect within the same channel keeps its position, battle and lesson state and merges progress lists; positions are saved only on logout and zone change (R-COST-002); a full channel (60) sends the player to the next channel of the zone.

## Why it is the best case

Shared tiles avoid griefing by body-blocking doorways; saving only on logout and warp is the cost rule; channels keep R-WORLD-001's cap.
