# DD-116: Obstinate costs two ability slots (slotCost 2, version 2) and a king ignores it: the captureFilter's list of restricted attackers excludes pieces of type 'king', so a king may move-capture an Obstinate piece of any rank while every other higher-rank attacker (rook on a knight or bishop, queen on anything below a queen) is still denied

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 5.1, 5.7, 5.8, 5.6, R-ABIL-005, R-RULES-001, R-RULES-004, DD-97, DD-102, plan item B5

## Decision

Obstinate costs two ability slots (slotCost 2, version 2) and a king ignores it: the captureFilter's list of restricted attackers excludes pieces of type 'king', so a king may move-capture an Obstinate piece of any rank while every other higher-rank attacker (rook on a knight or bishop, queen on anything below a queen) is still denied. Equal rank, effect captures, the Stalwart bypass (DD-102) and the reveal-on-observation rule (DD-99) are unchanged; the rules text reads 'pawn, then knight and bishop, rook, queen); kings ignore it'. Spec 5.7 and 5.8 rows updated; tests: module data expects 2 slots, 'R-RULES-001 DD-97 a king may capture an Obstinate piece: the king is not among the restricted attackers, a queen still is' added.

## Why it is the best case

docs/BALANCE_BASELINE.md section 5 rates Obstinate the strongest non-signature card in both formats: 88.9% on its own in First Blood (36 games, median 16 plies) and 95.8% in Full Battle (24 games, median 57), far above the 60% 'wins games by itself' bar, because with every piece immune to higher-rank captures the other side may attack only with pawns, knights and bishops and loses the first exchange (finding 7). Finding 2 calls it the backbone of every Full Battle build: the picker puts army-wide Obstinate in every Focused build except Storm's, and Storm without it loses 136 of its 200 non-mirror Full Battle games by checkmate (26%, 5-28% against four elements) while winning 97-100% of the same pairings in First Blood. Sections 9.3 and 10 name 'Obstinate at two slots' as the lever that narrows the per-type edge and Tide's kit (Hit and Run, Pierce, Obstinate), and the B1 note at the top of the baseline records that docs/BALANCE_DD98.md's picker had left passives out entirely, so the card had never been priced before. Two slots makes the shield cost a card instead of being free in a five-slot set. Letting kings ignore it keeps the DD-97 rank order intact (the king still ranks 5, so Obstinate on a king still protects nothing, as the simulator's inert rule assumes) while guaranteeing every army one piece that can always remove an Obstinate blocker standing next to the king, so a full Obstinate army can no longer be untouchable to a side that has lost its minor pieces.
