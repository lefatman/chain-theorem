# DD-103: Necromancer and Quantum Kill details: both consumable (1 and 2 charges, doubled by Overabundance, negated by Warden's Stopwatch where they revive); Necromancer revives on the starting square, else any empty back-rank square, never a king; Quantum Kill's effect capture respects Royal Immunity and its fallback bonus move is a non-capturing move by the captor

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 5.8, INV-07

## Decision

Necromancer and Quantum Kill details: both consumable (1 and 2 charges, doubled by Overabundance, negated by Warden's Stopwatch where they revive); Necromancer revives on the starting square, else any empty back-rank square, never a king; Quantum Kill's effect capture respects Royal Immunity and its fallback bonus move is a non-capturing move by the captor.

## Why it is the best case

Unlimited revives or removals on every favourable trade would dominate; the square fallback mirrors Rebirth's attuned rule (DD-22); INV-07 already forbids king removal.
