# DD-21: Riposte's bonus capture must be a legal move for the riposting side (never en passant) and must not leave the acting player's ordinary king in check (INV-03); if no such capture exists the bonus fizzles and the attuned fallback applies

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 5.7, INV-03, E6

## Decision

Riposte's bonus capture must be a legal move for the riposting side (never en passant) and must not leave the acting player's ordinary king in check (INV-03); if no such capture exists the bonus fizzles and the attuned fallback applies.

## Why it is the best case

INV-03 covers every ability effect from either side, and the fallback text says 'if none can'.
