# DD-42: A Hot Foot square that ignites during its igniter's opponent's own turn (a Riposte or other bonus capture moving the Ember piece off) is marked fresh: the rest of that turn does not count as one of the 3 opponent turns, and the flag clears when the turn ends

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: R-ELEM-005, DD-25

## Decision

A Hot Foot square that ignites during its igniter's opponent's own turn (a Riposte or other bonus capture moving the Ember piece off) is marked fresh: the rest of that turn does not count as one of the 3 opponent turns, and the flag clears when the turn ends. Burns lit on the igniter's own turn count as before.

## Why it is the best case

R-ELEM-005 says the square burns 'until the igniting player's opponent has taken 3 turns'; a partial turn in which the square was not yet burning is not a turn taken under the burn (review finding 9).
