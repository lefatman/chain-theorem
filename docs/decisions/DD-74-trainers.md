# DD-74: Trainers: a trainer's reward is a bonus on a win on top of battle XP; story trainers (once) pay it on the first win only and practice trainers on every win; quest defeat steps name story trainers

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 9.4, 10.2, 10.5

## Decision

Trainers: a trainer's reward is a bonus on a win on top of battle XP; story trainers (once) pay it on the first win only and practice trainers on every win; quest defeat steps name story trainers. The Academy road's required trainer fights First Blood at level 2; the optional route and meadow trainers fight Vanguard and are the session's one longer battle.

## Why it is the best case

Keeps the first win within reach of a new player (M5 done-when) and matches the 10.2 pacing of 4-6 First Blood battles and one longer battle per session.
