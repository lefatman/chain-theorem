# DD-72: Accepting a quest from its giver also counts as talking to the giver: a leading talk step naming that NPC completes at once

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 10.5, R-WORLD-005

## Decision

Accepting a quest from its giver also counts as talking to the giver: a leading talk step naming that NPC completes at once.

## Why it is the best case

The player has just spoken to that NPC; sending them back to repeat it is friction with no purpose (10.5).
