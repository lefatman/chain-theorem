# DD-32: Stalwart is revealed when its rule difference is observable: its owner makes a move that would be illegal for an ordinary king, a would-be checkmate is survived, the king is captured by a piece, or an effect that INV-03 would have fizzled resolves

Status: binding under D-37 (designer may overrule).
Spec: 4.3, 8.2

## Decision

Stalwart is revealed when its rule difference is observable: its owner makes a move that would be illegal for an ordinary king, a would-be checkmate is survived, the king is captured by a piece, or an effect that INV-03 would have fizzled resolves.

## Why it is the best case

8.2: a passive is revealed the first time its rule difference is observable.
